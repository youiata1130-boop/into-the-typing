# 蜂の女王

- 生成方法: 組み込みの `image_gen`。通常の蜂を参考画像にして、ボスの新規バリエーションを生成。
- 保存先: `src/assets/images/enemies/bee/boss/idle/frame_01.png`
- 参考画像: `src/assets/images/enemies/bee/level_1/idle/frame_01.png`
- 透過PNG。通常の蜂の画像は保持し、浮遊・攻撃・被弾・撃破はCSSで演出する。
- 2-3の最後に単独で登場。冠と赤いマントで通常の蜂と区別する。

## 使用プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent enemy boss sprite for a side-view Japanese fantasy typing RPG. Input image is the existing ordinary bee sprite, used as the style and species reference. Create a new boss variant: the Bee Queen, a single larger regal honeybee monster hovering and facing LEFT in three-quarter profile. Keep the reference's golden-yellow and dark brown striped body, big expressive dark eyes, fluffy cream collar, six small insect legs, pearly blue-white wings, polished anime cel shading and clean brown outlines. Make the queen unmistakably a boss with a small ornate gold crown with a ruby on her head between the antennae, a richer fluffy collar and a compact deep crimson royal mantle around the shoulders that leaves the striped abdomen and wings visible. Confident determined expression, child-friendly rather than frightening. Strong chunky silhouette readable at 150 pixels. Full creature with all wing tips, antennae, crown and small stinger completely in frame, landscape 3:2 canvas, centered with modest transparent margin. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No ground, scene, flowers, other bees, extra characters, human anatomy, text, UI, watermark, checkerboard, glow or painted shadows outside the creature. Preserve the existing ordinary bee file; this is a separate boss asset.
